# Light Bubbles

Bubbles stay bubbles: same flight, physics and merge math. But each one carries light, a small warm lantern
inside a water membrane, exhaled from her bell. This is a presentation-only change. There are **no changes to
`PlaneWorld`**: flight, hover, merging, items, damage and hit radii are untouched.

## Fiction (Sea-pedia)

> *Her bloom's light was too much for one bell to hold. What she cannot carry, she exhales — bubbles of light, each a
> little of everyone she lost, sent into the dark to do one bright thing before it goes out.*

The light is what washes corruption off. A bubble's glow passes over a creature; when its HP runs out, its own light
reignites, kindled by hers. Corrupted creatures flinch from light.

## What is built

| Part | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern core | `plane_bubble.gdshader` (`lantern`, `warmth`, `base_light`) | A hot gold centre (#FFC46B, the gonads' gold) and a soft warm fill through the film; the iridescent rim stays. Capped at `CORE_MAX` 1.12, below her bell's peak. |
| Brightness = damage | `CombatView.CoreLight` / `Warmth` | 0.32 for one bubble, ×1.35 per bubble merged in, capped at 1. Warmth goes from gold to white-gold over 1→15 bubbles. A full bubble's rainbow sheen becomes a faint corona around a small star. |
| Glow in the water | `bubble_halo.gdshader`, one pooled quad per shot | An additive billboard of a fixed 0.8 m radius. Its strength grows with merging, never its size: 0.16 → 0.4 (≤ 40 % of her halo). |
| Light on the world | `topdown_post.gdshader` (`bubble_lights[16]`, `bubble_count`) | Each bubble adds a 1.5 m halo on the swim layer at ≤ 0.35 of her halo strength. The 16 brightest within 25 m are sent; the rest glow on their own only. The halo uses her depth scaling (`0.6 + 0.8 × menace`), so it is subtle in the Shallows and strong in the deep. |
| Pop | `CombatView.Pop` | The lantern flares at ×1.5 for 150 ms and dies; droplets break off warm. It leaves a 1.5 m bloom of light that fades over 1 s (the same light array). |
| Her bell spends light | `BellView.Spend`, `clementine_bell.gdshader` `breath` | Each volley spends 0.07 per bubble of a light reserve, which rekindles at 0.45/s. The bell's interior glow (and her light) eases between 0.85 (spent) and 1.0. She is never dark. |
| Ammo orb | `BellView` charge orb | There are no ammo orbs on the plane yet. The orb that does exist, Pearl Diver's charge, is now the same lantern as her bubbles; a charged throw flies as a bright lantern bubble. |
| Merge feedback | `CombatView.Merged`, `Sfx` `merge` | A 200 ms light pulse at the merge point and a glassy chime, a semitone higher for every bubble the merged one holds (at most one chime per 60 ms). Bubbles hanging at the end of their range breathe slowly like embers. |
| Kill escalation | `CombatView.Kill`, `CameraRig.EdgeFlash`, `Sfx` `bell_ring` | The kill bloom's strength, life and reach (≤ 3 m) grow with the killing bubble's size. | Light returning home | `PufferlingView` freed poof | The freed fish's burst is little lanterns. For 0.5 s they drift toward Clementine (re-aimed as she moves), then rise away. |

## Guardrails (all enforced in code)

- A bubble's halo is ≤ 40 % of hers. Ground light radius is 1.5 m, and kill blooms are at most 3 m: brightness grows,
  radius does not.
- The kill flash lasts 60 ms, reaches only the screen's edges, and is off with reduced motion.
- Bubble cores are capped below her bell's peak, so she stays the warmest light.
- Bubble light scales with depth darkness like hers.

## Not built (phase 2)

- The corruption tell (§7): corrupted creatures darkening away from nearby bubble light, and healthy ones warming in
  it.

## Light shots: the bubble is gone

Her shots are now light itself. The sim still calls them bubbles, and every rule still applies: merging, multishot
and fans, bounces, boomerang, homing, splits, Starburst Gland, Pearl Diver, hovering, bait and dimming.

| Part | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Flight | `PlaneCombatTuning.ShotSpeed` 90 m/s, `BubbleRange` 12.8 m, `PlaneWorld.Extinguish`, `PlaneWorld.Reaches` | Light has no friction. It flies at full speed (90 m/s: it is light, so very fast; her full range in about 0.14 s) the way she aims, always to the end of her range, and goes out there like a match (`ShotOut`), never a step past it. Only the aim's way counts, never how near her the pointer or the crosshair is (the mouse pointer, the right stick's way; with the right stick left alone, the way she swims): anything in the way is struck anyway. Her range: 12.8 m with no pearls, half the screen's height at the 100% camera zoom (that framing shows 25.6 m of the swim band top to bottom), times her range stat. It never hovers or lingers (except with Bubble Coral: then it slows to a hover about 6 m out, well inside the view, hangs there for its hover time, and the shots that follow catch up and merge with it). It does not burst where it goes out, but its last light clears a little of the ground's corruption there, as a pop does (1.4 m, wider for a merged bubble). The Starburst Gland still bursts it into light. A shot that hits something still bursts on it. The view shows a last flicker, a few embers drifting up and cooling, and a faint curl of smoke. It flies 1.5 m a sim step, so what it meets is found along its whole flight since the last step (a creature, a throw, a sac, the boss: it bursts where it first touched it), and rock, a seal or a pot anywhere along that flight stops it: nothing small slips between two steps. |
| The mote | `light_shot.gdshader` on a camera-facing quad | A white-hot core, her gold glow whitening as it merges, and a slowly turning four-point twinkle. It grows slightly with merges (0.58 m to at most 1.5 m across) while its hit radius stays the sim's. Her shot size shows: the mote, its halo and its afterlight's width are all drawn at size^1.5 (`CombatView.DrawnSize`: ×1.3 draws ×1.48, from ×0.5 to ×3.2), so that even a small step is seen; a smaller shot is drawn smaller. The Starburst Gland makes it a little brighter, and Pearl Diver's throw and charge orb are a bigger, brighter mote. The halo and the swim-layer light stay. |
| Its pearls in it | `CombatView` (the mote's `motion`, `twin`, `points`, `spin`, `pearl`; `TrailStyle`) | Each shot pearl marks the light, so a build reads at a glance, and synergies combine the marks. **Swordfish Bill:** a lance, a white-hot spear along its heading (a short point ahead, a long tail behind), its afterlight straight, long and thin. **Mitosis:** two cores circling each other, joined by a membrane pinched in the middle like a dividing cell. **Starfish Arm:** a bold five-point star, and an afterlight as wide as the shot has grown. **Boomerang Shrimp:** its twinkle whirls, and its afterlight lasts longer (so the return arc shows). **Double Helix:** a second strand weaving round its afterlight. **Anglerfish Lure:** a lure's wisp, slow warm motes curling up off it, its afterlight softer and lingering. **Starburst Gland:** a fuse, sparks fizzing out to its sides, its bursts more often, its afterlight crackling wider. **Pistol Shrimp:** a string of cavitation bubbles torn open in its wake. **Pearl Diver:** a ring of nacre round the charged light, its lustre turning pink to gold, a wider afterlight. **Mirror Scale:** each bounce glints, a flash, a spray of sparks and a bloom. | Afterimage bursts | `CombatView.Shed` | The light sheds light as it flies. | Afterlight | `LightTrails`, `light_stroke.gdshader`, `light_wash.gdshader` | An afterimage, painted: each stroke is laid in three passes. First a warm amber wash glazed over the scene (mixed, not added, so it gives the light a painted edge even on the Shallows' pale sand, and glows amber in the dark). It fades over 0.32 s and briefly outlives the shot. It is jagged like lightning: every point is thrown aside and every segment kinked at its middle. The kinks re-strike 13 times a second and widen as the light ages. All trails and strikes are one mesh of two surfaces (the wash, the light), uploaded once a frame. |
| Impact | `CombatView.Firework` | A small firework: a white flash, then 16–60 sparks (more and faster for a merged shot). They are flung partly back from what was struck, drag to a stop and cool from white-gold to ember orange, while a few drift up. The pop's bloom of ground light stays. |
| Hit lightning | `LightTrails.Flares`, `CombatView.Flares` | When her light strikes something (a creature, a Blightroot, the boss, a vase), it is charged like a coil for a moment: bolts of lightning crackle straight out of its own outline, painted like the afterlight (wash, body, core). The harder the hit, the more, the longer and the longer-lasting (a plain shot's: about 5 bolts at a time, up to half a metre, for 0.36 s; up to 12, over a metre, for a hit four times as hard, at most 0.65 s).
