# The deeper fish: new corrupted fish for Depths 2–7

> **A proposal; only the Hearthguard is built.** Twelve new mobs, two to a depth from the Kelp Jungle down to the Tank, so each depth meets
> creatures of its own on top of the Shallows' three murklings and the Inkwarden. Every one is a reef fish (or, in the
> Abyss and the Tank, a tank fish) the Leak has rewritten. Each lives healthy on its depth's reef first, fills over ink
> like any fish (docs/ADVENTURE.md §3) and turns, and each is freed back into itself and swims off. The numbers are
> first guesses for play. §9 lists where they lean on the Theme Bible; §10 asks what to build.

## 1. The rules every one of them keeps

- **A fish first.** Its healthy form swims on its depth's reef, never a target, and eats ink dots like the others.
  Its gauge fills over ink (its own rates, §3–§7) and, full, it turns: the ink creeps over it for a moment, then it is
  the mob. A level starts with none of them corrupted, as now.
- **The corruption language** (THEME-BIBLE §6.4), all three tells, readable at 30 m:
  - **Wrong anatomy:** parts that do not belong, grown from inside. Never a face: nothing that glares or grins, no
    second mouth.
  - **The ink look:** a light-drinking black spreading from the mutation, a cold violet rim, the rest drained to ash.
  - **The drip:** ink drops welling up from the mutated places and lifting off upward.
- **How far it has gone follows Menace** (§6.6): at its first depth a touch of mutation, deeper down most of the body;
  speed, aggression and telegraphs ramp with it (telegraphs never under 0.35 s).
- **A corruption meter** like the murklings' (her shots, her arm, bursts empty it; holes tear and heal). It leaves an
  ink dot every 2.5 m it swims, like every corrupted creature.
- **Freed** (§6.5): it shudders, the mutation melts back into the body with the ink, the drip turns gold, a poof of
  bright bubbles, and it is its true self, smaller and whole. It swims off slowly, away from her, and once it is off
  the screen it is gone. It never turns again and never helps her.
- **Its own depth:** there it lives in its full numbers; every deeper depth keeps it at a lower rate
  (docs/ADVENTURE.md §3, "Each fish has its own depth").
- **Its own colour of light** for whatever it throws (the Theme Bible's fairness rule: creature shots glow in their own
  colour with short trails).

## 3. Depth 2 — Kelp Jungle

### Frondlurk (leafy seadragon)

- **Healthy:** a leafy seadragon drifting among the kelp, slowly, its leafy streamers swaying with the fronds. Lives
  only within kelp beds, 4–8 a level. Fills 0.08/s with a dot within 1 m.
- **Corrupted:** still the seadragon's shape, so in the kelp it passes for a frond: but its streamers have grown
  long and many, crowding along its back like a second fringe, and the ink spreads from their roots. **The drip gives
  it away**: a frond that drips upward. (This is the mob that teaches the third tell.)
- **Behaviour:** it hangs still in the kelp, turning to keep her in front of it. When she comes within 3.5 m, its
  streamers whip out in a lash along her side of it (wind-up 0.5 s: the streamers draw back and stiffen), reaching
  3.5 m: 8 damage and her pace held to 60% for 1.5 s (tangled). Then it drifts deeper into the kelp for 2 s before it
  can lash again. It never chases out of the kelp.
- **Counter:** spot the drip and shoot it from beyond 3.5 m; her arm or a dash through the kelp spoils its ambush.
- **Numbers:** 0.6 m long; meter as a spiked murkling's.

### Hearthguard (flounder) — built

A flounder lying on its patch (a reef shelf), shimmying into the sand; corrupted, a plain flat oval of ink that holds
its patch, flings short patterns of ink off its flanks as it shimmies, and draws its back up into a sting to stab her
when she comes close. Its numbers and behaviour are in docs/ADVENTURE.md §3–§4.

## 4. Depth 3 — Sunken Galleon

### Veilspine (lionfish)

- **Healthy:** a lionfish hovering by the wreck, its fins spread like a fan, slow. 4–6 a level, alone.
- **Corrupted:** its fan of fins grown huge and doubled, spines through them like ribs; ink sacs at the spine roots.
  (A quiet Sea-pedia fact, no wink: lionfish in far seas came from home aquariums.)
- **Behaviour:** within 12 m it turns its fan toward her: the fan is a wall across its front (120°) that **soaks up
  her shots**. It advances slowly (1.4 m/s), and every 3 s sheds a fan of five spines across its front (8 m/s, 6
  damage, its own pale violet), wind-up 0.6 s (the spines rise). Struck from the side or behind, it takes full damage
  and turns to face her again (90° a second).
- **Counter:** flank it: circle faster than it turns, or let her arm sting it from the side.
- **Numbers:** 0.9 m, the fan 2 m across; meter 1.5× a spiked murkling's.

### Wreckgulp (grouper)

- **Healthy:** a big grouper resting in a hole in the wreck or a cave mouth, barely moving. 2–3 a level.
- **Corrupted:** its gill covers grown into wide, frilled flaps; ink sacs bulge at its throat. Heavy, slow, dark.
- **Behaviour:** it waits in its hole. When she comes within 9 m in front of it, its gills flare (the telegraph,
  0.8 s), then it **inhales** for 1.5 s: a cone 9 m long, 60° wide, pulling her toward it at up to 4 m/s, and pulling
  in her shots (spent) and every ink dot in the cone. Reaching its mouth hurts (14) and spits her out. Then it
  **exhales**: the ink it drew in, as a cloud in front of it (dots, so it can make a lot of ink from a little).
  Rests 4 s.
- **Counter:** see the gills flare and dash sideways out of the cone; strike it while it rests. Clearing the ink round
  it first leaves it nothing to breathe back.
- **Numbers:** 1.6 m; meter 2.5× a spiked murkling's; never leaves its hole (it turns in it).

## 5. Depth 4 — Coral Carnival

### Mender (cleaner wrasse)

- **Healthy:** a small striped cleaner wrasse at its cleaning station on the coral, darting out to bigger fish and
  back. 3–4 stations a level, 1–2 at each.
- **Corrupted:** a second tail fin grows beside the first; the ink spreads from its stripe.
- **Behaviour:** it **never attacks**. It still believes it is cleaning: it darts (fast, erratic, 7 m/s) to the most
  wounded corrupted creature within 14 m and "cleans" it for 2 s: its holes close and its meter refills 25% a
  second. Then the next. Struck, it flees for a moment, then goes back to work.
- **Counter:** a priority target: small and quick, so shots that seek (homing pearls) and her arm shine here.
- **Numbers:** 0.25 m; meter half a spiked murkling's. At most one Mender per corrupted creature it can reach.
- **The No-Villain Test:** this is the clearest case of a neighbour rewritten: it is doing its job, for the wrong side.

### Cocoonback (parrotfish)

- **Healthy:** a parrotfish grazing the coral (a crunch you can hear), sleeping at night in a mucus bubble.
  4–6 a level.
- **Corrupted:** its beak-plates grown over each other in layers; ink sacs along its back.
- **Behaviour:** it grazes the rock, and every 2 s spits a short spray of ink grit at her if she is within 6 m (a fan
  of seven slow grains, 4 m reach, 3 damage each). Every 8 s, or when struck, it wraps itself in a **cocoon** of ink
  mucus: a shell that soaks up 3 shots (or one dash through it, or a charged pearl) before it pops; inside it, its meter
  heals. Popped, the cocoon bursts into slow ink bubbles (her shots burst them).
- **Counter:** pop the cocoon with a burst of fire, then strike it before it wraps again.
- **Numbers:** 0.8 m; meter 1.2× a spiked murkling's.

## 6. Depth 5 — Twilight Trench

### Dimmer (lanternfish)

- **Healthy:** a school of lanternfish (10–16), their photophores pale blue specks in the dark, drifting together.
  2–3 schools a level. They fill as a school, like chromis.
- **Corrupted:** their photophores gone black, swollen into little ink sacs; the school a cloud of ash-grey fish with
  violet rims.
- **Behaviour:** the school drifts toward her and hangs round her 4–7 m out. Each Dimmer within 7 m **drinks her
  light**: her lantern's reach (her searchlight and the clearing of the water round her) shrinks 4% for each, down to
  half at most, never out (Pillar 1). They brush her for 2 damage on contact, nothing more. The danger is what they
  hide: everything else in the Trench comes out of the dark.
- **Counter:** clear them and her light comes back. Freed, each lanternfish's specks glow blue again: the school
  swimming off is a little drift of lights in the dark, the Trench's freeing moment.
- **Numbers:** 0.12 m; meter a quarter of a spiked murkling's; one shot frees one.

### Darkdart (dragonfish)

- **Healthy:** a dragonfish hanging still in the dark, its chin barbel a tiny red light. 3–5 a level.
- **Corrupted:** its barbel grown long and branching, its light a cold violet spark; ink spreading from its jaw down
  its long body.
- **Behaviour:** it keeps just outside her light (her lantern's reach + 2 m), circling slowly; only the violet spark
  shows. Every 4–6 s it lunges: the spark flares (0.6 s, with a positional click), then it shoots straight through
  where she was at 14 m/s for 12 m: 12 damage on contact. Then it slows and swims back out into the dark.
- **Counter:** watch for the flare (and listen), sidestep, and shoot it as it slows after the lunge, when it is in her
  light.
- **Numbers:** 1.1 m; meter as a spiked murkling's.

## 7. Depth 6 — The Abyss

### Ribbonwrithe (oarfish)

- **Healthy:** an oarfish, a silver ribbon 12–20 m long with a red crest, winding slowly through the open water along
  the currents. 1–2 a level.
- **Corrupted:** its crest grown into a row of glossy ink sacs all along its back; ink spreading from them over the
  silver.
- **Behaviour:** it winds slowly (2 m/s) along a long loop through the level's open water. Its **body blocks her
  shots** and hurts on touch (6). Every 5 s the sacs burst one after another from head to tail, a **wave** of ink drops
  thrown out to both sides (each sac two drops, 7 m/s, 5 damage), travelling down its length over 2 s. Every part of
  it takes her shots into one shared meter; its head takes double.
- **Counter:** read the wave (it runs head to tail, so the tail end is the last to burst), slip through the gaps
  between waves, and shoot the head.
- **Numbers:** 18 segments of 1 m; meter 6× a spiked murkling's. Freed, the long silver ribbon swims off into the
  dark: the Abyss's biggest freeing.

### Wallgrazer (suckermouth catfish, a tank escapee)

- **Healthy:** none on the reef. The Theme Bible's lore (§4.1): the tank broke once and some fish escaped back to the
  sea, already changed. The Wallgrazers are those: they start corrupted, 2–4 a level, clinging to the walls. (The one
  exception to "none at the start", and the first creatures in the game that were never reef fish.)
- **Corrupted:** an armoured catfish, its bony plates grown over each other into a ridged shell; ink seeping from
  between the plates.
- **Behaviour:** it clings to rock walls at her plane and crawls along them (1 m/s), leaving ink on the wall's foot
  (a dot every metre). Its **armour turns her shots aside** while it clings. Every 8–12 s it lets go and swims across
  open water to another wall (3 m/s): crossing, its soft belly shows and it takes double damage. Touching it hurts (6).
- **Counter:** clear its ink behind it and catch it crossing.
- **Numbers:** 0.7 m; meter 2× a spiked murkling's. Freed, it is a plain brown catfish, and swims off into the dark.

## 8. Depth 7 — The Tank

The Tank's mobs are already Toy Diver and Snail (THEME-BIBLE §7). These two are its fish, in the Tank's flat light:
absurd, as the Tank is allowed to be (Pillar 6), never before it.

### Veilfighter (betta)

- **Healthy:** none: in the Tank everything is kept, and it is already corrupted, 2–3 in the Tank.
- **Corrupted:** a betta with fins grown far past the fish, veil upon veil, the ink spreading out through them.
- **Behaviour:** it spars with its own reflection in the glass (flaring at it, darting back), until she comes within
  8 m; then it turns on her and **flares**: its veils spread into a wide fan in front (wind-up 0.7 s), a sweep of ink
  across a 100° arc 4 m deep (10 damage). Between flares it chases at her cruise.
- **Counter:** stay out of the fan; strike from behind as it recovers (1 s).
- **Numbers:** 0.6 m, the veil 2.4 m; meter 2× a spiked murkling's.

### Bloatfin (fancy goldfish)

- **Healthy:** none: kept, overfed, already corrupted, 3–5 in the Tank.
- **Corrupted:** a fancy goldfish swollen round, its double tail grown into a tangle of fins; ink sacs where its
  bulging sides are.
- **Behaviour:** slow (1.5 m/s), it drifts toward the nearest fish-food flake (The Hand's flakes heal her) and
  **eats it first**. It bumps her aside (a soft shove, 2 damage). Every flake it eats makes it bigger and slower and
  its meter fuller.
- **Counter:** race it to the flakes, or free it first.
- **Numbers:** 0.8 m, growing to 1.4 m; meter as a spiked murkling's, +30% a flake.

## 9. Where they lean on the Theme Bible (to approve)

- **Names.** The Bible says creatures are species-true (§10); the adventure's corrupted creatures already have names
  of their own (Inkwarden, Lashjaw, the murklings). These follow the adventure: an invented name for the corrupted
  creature, the species for the fish (in the Sea-pedia: "Frondlurk — a leafy seadragon the Leak has rewritten").
- **Frondlurk hides in plain sight.** The Read-at-Range Test asks that a corrupted creature read as one at 30 m. Its
  shape passes for a frond; the drip still shows. It is the one mob that leans on the drip alone.
- **Dimmers dim her light.** Pillar 1: she never goes out. They shrink her lantern's reach to half at most; her bell
  and her glow stay as warm as ever (the Warmth Test holds).
- **Wallgrazers, Veilfighters and Bloatfins start corrupted** (no healthy form turns into them). They are tank fish:
  the lore's escapees in the Abyss, the Tank's own in the Tank.
- **Wreckgulp's inhale** moves her (a pull, not a camera break); the Two-Breaks Test is untouched.

## 10. Decisions

1. Which to build first. A suggestion: **Mender** and **Veilspine** (new kinds of play: a support to hunt, a facing
   to flank), then **Dimmer** (it ties the Trench's look to play), then the rest.
2. The names: keep them, or rename.
3. Whether the tank fish (Wallgrazer, Veilfighter, Bloatfin) may start corrupted.
