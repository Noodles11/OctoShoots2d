Ink Deep Cave Charts

Level maps from the new cave generator, drawn the way the minimap will draw them. Each tap of New level rolls a fresh seed; tap the full chart to move Clementine and the minimap follows her.

The rules each level meets

  • Reachable water covers 6,000 m², within 15% (5,100 to 6,900 m²). The 140 m square is only a container.
  • The start sits in a chamber on one side; the way down is the chamber at the end of the main chain, at least 90 m away along the water and 78 m in a straight line.
  • Exactly one place lies 39 to 47 m from the start; every other place is farther, at least 40 m from the way down and 21 m from the next place. Rooms prefer dead-end chambers.
  • Boss levels end in a round 15 m arena with the Crack; other levels in a blue hole.
